How these numbers are calculated

  • BMI = weight ÷ height². Classes follow WHO (18.5 / 25 / 30 / 35).
  • Body fat % = 1.20·BMI + 0.23·age − 10.8·(male) − 5.4 (Deurenberg et al., 1991). Typical error against DXA is ±4–5 percentage points; it can't see whether you carry more muscle than average.
  • Lean mass = weight − fat mass. Muscle is a rough share of lean mass (52% male, 47% female), shown for trends only.
  • Body water assumes lean tissue is 73% water (Wang et al., 1999).
  • FFMI / FMI = lean or fat mass ÷ height² (Kouri 1995; VanItallie 1990); normalised FFMI adjusts to 1.8 m.
  • BMR: Mifflin–St Jeor (1990). Ideal weight: Devine, Robinson, Miller, Hamwi; optimal here = BMI 22.
  • Fat categories: ACE ranges; healthy bands: Gallagher et al. (2000), by age and sex.
  • The scale's raw electrical impedance is stored but not used, because its vendor algorithm is unpublished.
Informational only, not medical advice. Day-to-day weight swings of 1–2 kg from water and food are normal, so judge progress by the trend rather than single readings.
